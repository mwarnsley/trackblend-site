TrackBlend

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

The short version

  • TrackBlend does not collect, track, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your card or billing details.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

Information we do not collect

TrackBlend does not ask for, collect, track, sell, or transmit personally identifiable information. There is no account to create, and we never request your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise or approximate location. TrackBlend contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no cross-app tracking.

Data stored on your device

Everything the game needs to remember is saved locally on your device and is never uploaded to us. That includes:

  • Game progression: completed levels, stars, crowns, and best times
  • Unlocked and selected themes, and Train Yard decorations
  • Daily Express streaks and results
  • Audio settings (soundtrack, volumes), haptics, accessibility, and reminder preferences
  • Hint tokens and the tracks you have drawn

Deleting TrackBlend removes this data. If you use iCloud or device backups, your device may include it in those backups under Apple's own privacy terms.

In-app purchases (TrackBlend Pro)

TrackBlend Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. Your payment method, credit card number, and billing details are handled by Apple and never touch our systems.

To confirm that a purchase is valid and to let you restore it, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-verification service. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID, the App Store receipt for your purchase, and basic technical details such as the app version and device platform. This information is not linked to your name or contact details, is used only to verify and restore purchases, and is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

Notifications

If you turn on daily puzzle reminders, TrackBlend schedules local notifications on your device. No push token or notification data is sent to us, and you can turn reminders off at any time in Settings.

Children's privacy

TrackBlend is suitable for players of all ages. Because the game does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
